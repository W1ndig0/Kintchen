KITCHEN HUB 9.8.5 - 56 NEW THINGS, AND WHAT EACH ONE IMPROVES
==============================================================
Inspiration: MagInkDash and Timeframe (e-ink family dashboards), FamQuest (fridge chores), Hanselman's wall
calendar, "keepalive" Actions, ntfy, Apple Shortcuts. Everything below is built and tested here; the TV and
Siri parts depend on the Apple apps described in GITHUB_SETUP.txt / SHORTCUTS.txt.

THE CLOUD BRAIN (GitHub Actions, every 15 min, free)
 1. Cloud data bundle - weather, news, what's on, recipes and horoscopes fetched on GitHub; the Kindle downloads
    ONE file instead of ~25: a few seconds of Wi-Fi instead of a minute-plus of fetching -> battery.
 2. Falls back to fetching directly if the bundle is missing or over 2 h old - never worse than 9.8.
 3. Your news sources and markets list (fridge Settings) travel to the cloud with the fridge's check-in.
 4. No quiet hours in the cloud: night-time news is fresh in the morning without the fridge spending battery.
 5. Morning briefing push (7 am): weather, shift, events, chores, tonight's dinner, list count.
 6. "Tomorrow: shift 10:45-2:45p" push at 8 pm, with tomorrow's weather.
 7. "Shift at 10:45am - starts in 55 min, it's -4 out" push.
 8. Event reminder an hour before (events with a time).
 9. Chore pushes at their time (garbage night!).
10. Birthdays/anniversaries: a week ahead and the day before.
11. "Tomorrow is Family Day" stat-holiday heads-up (store hours).
12. Sunday 6 pm: your week ahead in one push.
13. Saturday: "12 things on the list - shopping day?"
14. Watchdog: "The fridge hub went quiet" if it misses its check-ins (dead battery, Wi-Fi, crash).
15. Low-battery push from the cloud as a backup to the fridge's own reminder.
16. New Environment Canada warnings for Prince George pushed once each.
17. Meal plan: fills empty dinners for the next 7 days from your saved recipes + the recipe feeds; never
    touches a day you set or cleared.
18. Recipe import from any link (JSON-LD recipe card) - from the phone, PC, or the iPhone share sheet.
19. Calendar feed (.ics): shifts with real start/end times, events with a 30-min alert, chores as true repeats,
    B.C. stat holidays - subscribe on iPhone, Outlook, Google Calendar.
20. TV data, locked with your passphrase.
21. Siri/PC inbox: plain "milk, eggs" lines become locked list items within minutes.
22. Re-locks anything that arrived unlocked.
23. "Test phone push" request from the phone app.
24. Keep-alive: GitHub stops schedules after 60 quiet days; the brain commits one tiny file a month so it never dies.
25. Writes only when something changed (fewer gist revisions; the fridge's check stays a tiny 304).
26. Update manifest built automatically when you upload new fridge files.

THE FRIDGE (Kindle)
27. Updates itself from your GitHub site: every file checksum-verified, all-or-nothing, then it restarts itself.
    No more USB after this one. (Tested: a tampered file aborts the update and nothing changes.)
28. Fridge notes from the phone/PC/Siri appear in the bulletin strip and on the standby front page; tap = read
    (the phone sees "read").
29. Remote control from the phone/PC: timer, sync now, check/install update, upload log, front page/tiles,
    contrast, open a screen, reload. Each runs once and reports back.
30. Repeating chores in every calendar view (tile, day sheet, full calendar), marked with their rule.
31. Tonight's planned dinner on the dinner screen and the standby front page.
32. Check-in (kindle.json): battery, 7-day drain, hours left, version, last sync - for the phone, TV and brain.
33. Log upload (daily + on demand) to your cloud gist: you can send me logs without a cable.
34. Settings backup/restore: your layout and every Feel setting, locked in the gist; one tap after a reinstall.
35. Reads the Siri/PC inbox itself at every sync, so "add milk" shows up at the next wake.
36. Settings > Phone > GitHub cloud: brain status, update button, log button, backup/restore,
    calendar QR (scan to subscribe) and TV QR.
37. "Data from the cloud" on/off switch.

THE PHONE APP
38. FRIDGE tab: battery %, days/hours left, last check-in (warns if quiet), version, cloud-data status.
39. Pin a note on the fridge.
40. Remote control buttons (timers, sync, update, log, screen, contrast, reload).
41. Read the fridge's log right on the phone.
42. Chores editor: weekly (any days), every 2 weeks, monthly, last day of month, yearly, daily - optional time.
43. Meal plan: this week's dinners, tap a day to change it, x to clear, re-roll the week.
44. Import a recipe from a link.
45. Subscribe-to-calendar and TV links (open/copy).
46. Test-push button. Also fixed: the add-item bar no longer floats over other tabs; 5 tabs fit an iPhone.

THE TV (Apple TV / any big screen)
47. Living-room dashboard in the almanac style: Today (big clock, notes, shift, events, dinner, weather, alerts,
    list), Week (7 columns with shifts, events, chores, dinners), Kitchen (list, chores, dinner plan),
    News (+ what's on in P.G.), Fridge (battery, On This Day, markets, new recipes).
48. Cook on the TV: down arrow -> pick a recipe -> ingredients -> big steps with the remote.
49. Night mode 11 pm-6 am (just the clock and tomorrow); dark colours in the evening, light by day (D toggles).
50. Burn-in care (the page drifts a few pixels), auto-refresh every 5 min, offline-tolerant.
51. Optional photo frame slide from photos/ (public - read the warning).

THE PC
52. kh command line: add, list, done, note, timer, event, chore, recipe, plan, status, log, tv, cal, sync,
    update, remotelog - same encryption as the fridge (self-tested before every write).
53. "kh tv" opens the dashboard - perfect second-monitor view (F11).
54. Desktop notifications via ntfy in the browser (timers, shifts, briefing).

SIRI / APPLE WATCH / HOMEPOD
55. "Hey Siri, add to fridge list" / "leave a fridge note" / "fridge timer" (also from the Watch and HomePod).
56. Share any recipe page from Safari to the hub; NFC tag on the fridge door; arrive-at-the-store automation.
