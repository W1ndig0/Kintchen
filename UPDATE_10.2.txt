KITCHEN HUB 10.2 - AUTO-UPDATE WITH ROLLBACK, A WEB READER, MORE EVERYWHERE
Install: upload the kit to GitHub like before (everything, including the .github folder), then on the fridge
Settings > Phone > CHECK FOR UPDATE (tap twice). This is the last update you have to install by hand:
from now on the fridge updates itself at night, and undoes an update that doesn't start properly.

AUTO-UPDATE + ROLLBACK
 1  The fridge checks your GitHub site once a night (after 2 am) and installs new versions by itself.
 2  Settings > Phone > Updates: "Only when I tap", "Tell me" or "At night" (the default).
 3  "Tell me": the news strip says "Update X is ready - tap to install", and your phone gets a push.
 4  Every update first saves a copy of each file it replaces.
 5  A new version is on probation until its page reports in after starting.
 6  If it never reports in (two whole wakes), the fridge goes back to the previous version by itself.
 7  If a new script won't even parse, it's undone before anything starts.
 8  A version that had to be undone is never installed again by itself.
 9  GO BACK TO THE PREVIOUS VERSION button in Settings > Phone (tap twice).
10  Phone app (Fridge tab): update status, plus an "Undo last update" button.
11  PC: "kh rollback" and "kh version".
12  Phone pushes: "Fridge hub updated to X" once it starts fine, or "Fridge update undone" if it didn't.
13  On GitHub, every upload is checked first (scripts parse, the page runs on the Kindle's old browser).
    A broken upload gets a red X and the fridge never sees it.
14  WHAT'S NEW: the notes for the new version show on the fridge once, after it updates
    (and any time from Settings > Phone).

WEB READER (Apps > Web)
15  Type a web address or some search words (searching uses DuckDuckGo).
16  Pages show as clean, large text sized for e-ink. The site's own scripts and pictures never load, so
    heavy sites still read fine.
17  Links are underlined with a number: tap one to follow it.
18  BACK, RELOAD, and MORE for long pages.
19  SAVED: sites that read well on e-ink are already there (NPR text, CNN Lite, CBC Lite, Wikipedia,
    Environment Canada for Prince George, Hacker News). Use SAVE to add the page you're on.
20  HISTORY of the pages you opened.
21  Phone app: "Send a web page to the fridge" (Fridge tab) opens a recipe or article on the fridge.
22  PC: "kh web <address or search words>".
23  SEND TO PHONE pushes the page you're reading to your phone.
24  KINDLE BROWSER opens the Kindle's own browser for pages that need it; the hub comes back at the
    next wake.
    (Firefox can't run on this Kindle: it needs a far newer Linux, graphics stack and more memory than the
    Paperwhite has. The reader does the job on the hub itself.)

TV
25  New HOME slide: today's chores (ticked or not), bills this week, food to use up, flyer deals and the
    joke of the day.

PC TOOL
26  "kh spend 42.50 groceries" logs a purchase.
27  "kh food chicken 2" adds food with a best-before date.

FIXED
28  Long pages (pantry, money, the web reader) skipped any row taller than the screen, so you could never
    see it. Now it's shown, cut at the bottom.
29  Guest Wi-Fi BIG CODE: a long network name or password could still run past the bottom in landscape.
30  The Kindle sync tests now also cover the update and rollback steps (backup, health, undo, skipping a
    bad version).
