KITCHEN HUB 9.9.1 - BIG VIEW FIXES + SPEED
Install: upload the kit to GitHub like before, then on the fridge Settings > Phone > CHECK FOR UPDATE (tap twice).

FIXED
 1  Tapping a tile opened the big view and then it snapped back small, so it took a second tap.
    Cause: anything that re-packs the tiles (a sync finishing, new data, the time of day changing the
    layout) shrank the tile back while the hub still thought it was open. The big view now survives that.
 2  The tap that opens the big view could also "press" the button that appears under your finger
    (PREV/NEXT/CLOSE sit where small tiles at the bottom were). Taps in the first 0.3 s are ignored.
 3  Tiles that are already big (half the screen or more) skip the big view and open on the first tap.
 4  Big view formatting: tiles now draw their large layout (more rows, the forecast strip, etc.),
    text grows to use the space, and nothing hides behind the button bar.
 5  Shifts tile: the pay line was added again on every refresh (showed twice or more). Fixed, and it's
    hidden when the period has no hours yet.
 6  The "OPEN >" tag no longer shows inside the big view (the bar has OPEN).

FASTER
 7  The list/calendar data was re-read and re-decrypted dozens of times per screen refresh. It's now
    read once per change - fewer pauses after a tap and less battery while awake.
 8  "Most bought" chips were recounted on every list redraw; now only when the list changes.
 9  A doubled calendar + bulletin refresh after every sync removed.
