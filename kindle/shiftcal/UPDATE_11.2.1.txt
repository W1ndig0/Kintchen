KITCHEN HUB 11.2.1 - A FULL BUG HUNT
Installs by itself from your PC: Settings > Updates > CHECK FOR UPDATE (the PC part needs "Install a kit file" first).
Everything from 11.0 to 11.2 gone over again, looking for faults. These were found and fixed.

SAFETY
 1  Phones that reach the PC through "tailscale serve" were treated as the PC itself (no pairing needed). They now
    pair like any other device. A web page open in the PC's browser can no longer talk to the hub as the owner.
 2  The fridge's key only opens what the fridge needs: it can no longer make pairing codes, list or remove devices, or run jobs.

FIXES
 3  A big book (a long PDF) is sent in 1.5 MB parts instead of one piece, so the Kindle's small temporary memory is not overrun.
 4  One book file that is still being copied or is open in another program no longer stops the whole book folder scan.
 5  Ten bad requests spread over weeks no longer lock a device out for 15 minutes (a good request clears the count).
 6  A kit that loads but keeps crashing is dropped after 3 starts in a row, and the built-in copy runs (no endless restart loop).
 7  The price check can no longer get stuck, and a second ask that comes in during a check is answered too.
 8  A job run that cannot start no longer leaves the PC thinking it is still running.
 9  "Get the newest kit" never waits for a password prompt nobody can see.
10  Push to the iPhone uses a contact address Apple accepts (can be changed with "pushSubject" in config.json).
11  The fridge says so when its clock is more than 5 minutes off the PC's, instead of "pair again".
12  The fridge's pairing hint no longer talks about editing cloud.conf.

NOTES
13  Windows copies of the kit keep their scripts with the right line endings (.gitattributes).
14  README: the PC must not sleep and should have a fixed address in the router - those are the two reasons the fridge says "the PC didn't answer".
