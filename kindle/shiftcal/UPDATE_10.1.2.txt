KITCHEN HUB 10.1.2 - BUG SWEEP: 61 FIXES
Install: upload the kit to GitHub like before (everything, including the .github folder), then on the fridge
Settings > Phone > CHECK FOR UPDATE (tap twice). The phone app updates itself next time you open it.
If you use the PC tool, copy the new pc/kh.ps1 over your old one.

How these were found: lint and static checks on every file, a stress test that opens every fridge page on
both screen sizes in both orientations with odd data (very long names, HTML in names, broken dates, empty
cloud files, garbage weather), a midnight / month-end / New Year clock test, a fuzz test of the cloud
brain and phone app with damaged records, and a read-through of the pay, calendar, inbox and update code.
You asked for 200: these are the 61 that were real. I didn't pad the list.

SHIFTS + PAY
 1  "6-2" (also "6-2p", "5-1") was read as 6 pm-2 am, or as a 20-hour shift. Now 6 am-2 pm.
 2  "4-12" was read as 4 pm to noon the next day. Now it ends at midnight.
 3  The fridge, the pay maths and the phone calendar each had their own shift reader and could disagree.
    They now share one.
 4  Phone calendar: an overnight shift like "23-7" ended at 7 pm the same day.
 5  Weekly overtime only counted hours inside the pay period. A period that starts mid-week (the 1st or
    16th) forgot that week's earlier hours and under-counted overtime.
 6  Easter Monday and Boxing Day were paid as stat holidays (1.5x). They aren't B.C. stats.
 7  Days off: the fridge only understood "off". "vac", "sick", "stat" and "x" showed as shifts (next-shift
    line, both-off days, TV). The fridge, TV and cloud now share one list.

MIDNIGHT + DATES
 8  At midnight only the calendar redrew. Pantry, money, pay, shifts, chores and the deals countdown kept
    yesterday's "today/tomorrow" until the next sync.
 9  A record with a broken date showed "undefined NaN" on money, pantry, package and goal rows.
10  Reminders every N months or every year now name the date ("every year on Oct 15").
11  The fridge calendar only knew chores 3 weeks back and 3 months ahead. Now 6 weeks back and 5 months ahead.
12  A remote "timer 10" sent while the fridge slept started a full 10-minute timer hours later. Now it runs
    only what's left, or skips it.

PHONE CALENDAR FEED
13  "Every N months" chores went out with a blank repeat rule, which some calendar apps reject (dropping
    the whole feed).
14  "Take turns" chores were in nobody's calendar. Now each of you gets your own weeks.
15  Bills and chores on the 29th-31st skipped short months in calendar apps (the fridge moves them to the
    last day; the feed now does too).
16  Events and chores at 11 pm had no length.

SIRI / PC INBOX
17  If both the fridge and the cloud read the same inbox note, every item was added twice.
18  A command that didn't make sense ("timer: -5", "event: tomorrow lunch") went onto the shopping list
    as text. That happened in the cloud and on the fridge.
19  "event: 2026-11-20 3:30pm Dentist" (am/pm) put "3:30pm" in the title with no time. Impossible dates
    made broken events.
20  A "|" in an item split it into the wrong fields on the fridge.

PC TOOL (kh.ps1)
21  "kh done" with nothing after it ticked off the whole shopping list.
22  Writes didn't check they landed. If the phone or fridge saved at the same moment, one change could
    vanish. It now verifies and retries.
23  "kh timer" with no number queued a 0-minute timer.
24  "kh event" took times like 25:99 and events with no title.
25  "kh chore" refused the every-N-months rule.
26  "kh plan" crashed on an odd plan line.

DEALS + FLYERS (cloud)
27  More than 12 watched items: items 13+ were never price-checked. Now the longest-waiting go first.
28  More than 20 list items: items 21+ were never flyer-checked, and each check forgot earlier matches.
29  A Flipp outage, or one failed lookup, wiped every known sale from the fridge.
30  A sale that ended stayed on the fridge until the next 6-hour check.
31  Recipe ideas were wiped by a TheMealDB outage.
32  Black Friday week was worked out in the wrong time zone (cloud and phone).
33  Game reminders were skipped if the NHL had already marked the game "pre-game".
34  The "Use it up" push listed food that expired weeks ago, every evening. Now only today, tomorrow and
    the last 2 days.
35  "The fridge hub went quiet" could push at 3 am. It now waits for morning.

CLOUD BRAIN
36  A damaged brain.json or extras file crashed every run (or switched deals off for good). It now starts
    fresh.
37  The list never shrank. Finished commands and notes, chore ticks, bill ticks and old meal plans piled
    up forever, and the fridge decrypts every line on each sync. The brain now tidies once a day.
38  Settings saved as something other than {...} crashed the money, waste and fun readers (fridge, phone,
    cloud).

FRIDGE SCREENS
39  The fridge keyboard had no capitals control and no symbols, so a Wi-Fi password like "HomeNet#24!"
    couldn't be typed (the guest Wi-Fi code would be wrong). Added Aa / abc / ABC and a #+= page.
40  Apps page: a "<" in a calendar event title broke the Calendar card (it was read as HTML).
41  Weather: a partial answer from the weather service showed "NaN°" everywhere. Now the last good
    forecast stays.
42  The weather page crashed on a forecast with missing hourly data.
43  News: a story with no title or source showed as "2undefined".
44  Recipes: one broken entry in the recipe feed blanked the whole list.
45  Recipes: an entry with no source showed an "undefined" label; a numeric title crashed the reader.
46  Cloud data with missing pieces (no store name, no team record, empty trivia, bad game time) printed
    "undefined". All cloud data is now cleaned when it loads.
47  A flyer with no store name read "at undefined".
48  The waste chart drew broken bars if a count was damaged, and tossing then added text to the count.
49  Long words (Wi-Fi passwords, links) ran off the side of pages. They wrap now.
50  Guest Wi-Fi BIG CODE ran past the bottom of the screen in landscape.
51  Long pages (pantry, money): page 2 could show a FRIDGE or Recent heading with no rows under it.
52  Calendar: with the filter on the roommate, removing the roommate hid all your own shifts.
53  Money: "you're ahead of it" (vs last month) was unclear. It now says how much more or less you've
    spent so far.
54  The settings backup to your gist practically never ran (its 6-hour timer freezes while the Kindle
    sleeps). It now checks the real clock each awake minute.
55  Settings restore accepted any part of a real setting's name and wrote junk into the Kindle's storage.
56  An old, unused shift pop-up was still wired to two functions that no longer exist. Removed.

PHONE
57  With no signal, opening the TV page showed the phone app instead. The TV page is now saved for
    offline use too.
58  An every-2-weeks or yearly chore saved with the date box empty became a broken chore.
59  "Tossed" counted wrong if the waste number had been damaged.

UPDATES
60  If the Kindle ran out of space halfway through an update, it could be left with half old, half new
    files. Every file is now copied and checked before any is switched.
61  The fridge's own inbox reader treated an empty "note:" as a shopping item.
