KITCHEN HUB 10.0 - DEALS, MONEY, FOOD, HOME, FUN
Install: upload the kit to GitHub like before (drag everything, including the .github folder), then on
the fridge Settings > Phone > CHECK FOR UPDATE (tap twice). Deals and flyers fill in after the next cloud
brain run (within 15 min; Best Buy every 3 h, flyers every 6 h).
New tiles are off by default: Edit tiles (grid button) > tap the tray chips DEALS / MONEY / FOOD / FUN.
Every tile opens a full page with tabs; the same things are on the phone under DEALS and HOME.

DEALS (fridge tile + phone DEALS tab)
 1  Wishlist: add anything ("air fryer", "PS5 controller"); the cloud brain checks Best Buy Canada for it.
 2  Shows the current Best Buy price, the regular price and how much it's marked down.
 3  Optional target price per item - set it from the fridge or the phone.
 4  Push to your phone when an item hits your target price.
 5  Push when a wishlist item drops 10% or more since the last check.
 6  Price history kept per item (lowest seen is shown).
 7  Sale end date shown when Best Buy gives one.
 8  Grocery flyers for V2N (Prince George) from Flipp: Save-On, Walmart, Real Canadian Superstore, etc.
 9  Flyer search runs for everything on your shopping list, so you see who has it on sale this week.
10  Flyer price tag next to list items that are on sale somewhere ("$3.99 Walmart").
11  Flyer "sale story" (e.g. "2 for $5", "Save $2") and valid-until date.
12  Thursday push: "N things on your list are on sale this week" (new flyers drop Thursday).
13  Hot deals feed from RedFlagDeals (Canada) - top deals, refreshed hourly.
14  Push when a hot deal matches something on your wishlist.
15  Black Friday week and Boxing week: a banner on the hot deals page, and deals are checked every 20 min.
16  "+ WATCH" on any hot deal puts it on your wishlist in one tap.
17  Deals tile summary: best markdown, count on sale, count of list items in flyers.
18  Phone DEALS tab: add wishlist items, set targets, open the product page.

MONEY (fridge tile + phone HOME tab)
19  Log spending in two taps: amount, then a category chip (groceries, eating out, gas, fun, home, other).
20  Who paid (you / roommate) on every expense.
21  "Settle up": shows who owes who and how much, and records the payback.
22  Monthly budgets per category, with a bar on the tile.
23  Push when a category passes 90% of its budget.
24  Bills: name, amount, due day of the month (rent, phone, internet, hydro...).
25  Bills due in the next week show on the front page and the tile.
26  Push 2 days before a bill is due, and again if it's overdue.
27  Mark a bill paid from the fridge; it comes back next month by itself.
28  Bills on autopay are marked AUTOPAY and don't nag you.
29  Savings goals with a target and a progress bar; add money to a goal from the fridge or phone.
30  This-month view: total spent, by category, by person, and the bigger expenses.
31  Pay period estimate (from 9.9) now sits next to the month's spending so you can see what's left.
32  Repeat rule "every N months" for things like car insurance or quarterly bills.

FOOD
33  Pantry: what's in the fridge/freezer with an optional use-by date.
34  Leftovers: one tap logs "leftovers" with a 4-day use-by.
35  Use-it-up list on the front page: things expiring today or tomorrow.
36  Morning push for anything that has to be used today.
37  "Used" / "Tossed" buttons; tossed items are counted as waste.
38  Waste tracker: how many things were thrown out this month vs last (on the pantry page).
39  "To list": a finished pantry item goes straight back onto the shopping list.
40  Food tile: next item to use up plus counts.
41  Meal plan "tonight" line stays on the Food page too.

HOME
42  Chores with rotation: "take turns" alternates you and your roommate week by week.
43  Chore list shows whose turn it is next, with "(alternates)" on take-turns chores.
44  Upkeep presets in one tap: furnace filter, fridge coils, smoke alarm test, dryer vent, water filter,
    deep clean - each with a sensible repeat.
45  Packages: track what's coming (name + carrier); shows on the front page until you mark it received.
46  Packages line in the morning briefing.
47  Guest Wi-Fi card: network and password in big text on the fridge, so guests can read it.
48  Phone HOME tab: chores, bills, spending, pantry and packages in one place.
49  Phone chore editor: "Whose job" now has "Take turns".

FUN (fridge tile)
50  Trivia question of the day (Open Trivia DB) - tap to reveal the answer, NEXT for another.
51  Word of the day (Merriam-Webster) with its meaning.
52  Canucks: next game, opponent, time (BC time), home/away, and last result.
53  Game-day line in the morning briefing.
54  Fun tile shows the word, today's question and the next Canucks game at a glance.

CLOUD BRAIN
55  New deals/extras step: Best Buy, Flipp, RFD, trivia, word, NHL - each on its own timer so GitHub
    minutes stay low.
56  Everything it finds goes into an encrypted extra.js in your cloud gist (same passphrase).
57  extra.js is only rewritten when something changed (less noise, fewer Kindle downloads).
58  The fridge and the phone both read it; the Kindle fetches it with the normal sync.
59  Morning briefing now includes bills due, use-today food, packages, game day and flyer matches.
60  Postal code for flyers can be changed: repo Settings > Secrets and variables > Actions > Variables,
    add POSTAL (default V2N4Z9).

FRIDGE PAGE
61  New full-page overlay with tabs for Deals, Money, Food, Home and Fun (big text, BACK button).
62  Quick chooser chips for categories/people instead of typing.
63  New icons for the four new tiles and the apps page.
64  The apps page has entries for Deals, Money, Food, Home and Fun.
65  Front page: bills-due and use-it-up lines.
66  New tiles re-draw when the list or money changes (no waiting for the next sync).
67  Each wishlist item has a small price-trend line.
68  Bills page shows OVERDUE / DUE TODAY / TOMORROW / IN 2 DAYS tags.
69  All new data is shared with the phone and TV through the same encrypted list (last change wins).
