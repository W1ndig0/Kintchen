KITCHEN HUB 9.8
===============
Install: copy the shiftcal folder over /mnt/us/extensions/shiftcal (your cloud.conf,
hub.tsv and settings are kept), then run Kitchen_Hub_Display.sh from Documents as usual.

FIXED
- The tiny clock. The page is rotated with CSS, and the "does it fit?" check was reading
  the wrong screen edges, so the clock always shrank to its smallest size. The check now
  follows the rotation, and the clock (tile, standby front page, night screen) is sized from
  its measured width instead. Each size is written to power.log as "page: clock ..." -
  please send power.log once if it still looks small.

NEW
- Recipes: SEARCH tab. Type anything (or tap an idea like Chili); the hub turns on Wi-Fi,
  searches RecipeTin Eats, Budget Bytes and Cookie + Kate, and shows the results in about a
  minute. Results open in the same reader as NEW recipes.
- "+ ADD TO SHOPPING LIST" on every recipe (reader and the cook-mode ingredients page).
  Amounts and units are stripped ("2 cups flour, sifted" -> Flour), salt/pepper/water are
  skipped, and anything already on the list isn't added twice.
- Automatic screens (Settings > Feel, each can be turned off or retimed):
    Shift morning - 1-3 h before a shift: countdown, weather, road/weather alerts, today's events.
    Dinner (4-7 pm) - a recipe idea, the shopping list, RECIPES and quick timer buttons.
    Night (10 pm-6 am) - while asleep: big clock, tomorrow's shift + events, low-battery note.
  They appear only after 45 s untouched; a tap dismisses one for the rest of the day.
- Settings > Feel: Contrast (Normal/Strong/Max), Text weight (Normal/Bold), the three screens,
  News sources (CKPG, CBC BC/Canada/World/Business/Tech) and topic mutes
  (sports, showbiz, crime, politics, U.S.).
- Battery: a week of history. Settings > System shows the drain over the whole week, hours/days
  left and roughly when it will be empty. Your phone gets ONE ntfy alert when about a day is left
  (resets when you charge).

BATTERY / SYNC SAVINGS
- Night (midnight-6 am): news every 3 h only, no recipe fetch, no markets.
- Markets only on weekdays 6 am-2 pm; On This Day once a day; What's On feeds every 3 h
  (alerts every wake); recipes every 6 h.
