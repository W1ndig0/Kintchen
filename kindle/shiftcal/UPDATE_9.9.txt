KITCHEN HUB 9.9 - PEOPLE, PAY AND A BIG REFRESH
===============================================
INSTALL (no USB)
  1. Upload the contents of kitchen_hub_9.9_github_kit.zip to your repo, the same way as last time
     (Add file > Upload files > drag everything in > Commit). It replaces the old files.
  2. Wait ~1 minute (GitHub rebuilds the fridge's update list), then on the fridge:
     Settings > Phone > CHECK FOR UPDATE, then tap it again to install. The hub restarts by itself.
     (Or from your phone: FRIDGE > Install update.)
  3. Phone: open the app and pull to refresh once.
  4. Your roommate: Settings > People > Person 2 > type his name. Then Settings > Phone shows HIS
     calendar code and HIS phone pairing code (they appear after the next cloud brain run, ~15 min).
  Pay: Settings > Pay. Your rate is already $18.25/h. Set "A pay period started on" to the first day of
  one of your real pay periods, and when payday comes.
