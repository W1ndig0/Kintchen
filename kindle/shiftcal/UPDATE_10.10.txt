KITCHEN HUB 10.10 - QUICK, QUIET AND EASY ON THE BATTERY
Installs by itself like any update: Settings > Updates > CHECK FOR UPDATE.

SYNC
 1  The fridge only goes online when you tap SYNC. Waking it with the button or a tap no longer syncs, and
    nothing on the page fetches anything by itself. Everything from the cloud (weather, news, markets,
    birds, deals) arrives together in one package.
 2  An edit on the fridge (a list item ticked, added or removed) goes up right away in a short burst: Wi-Fi
    on for 20 s at most, lists only. Settings > Sleep > "Send fridge edits" can hold them for the next
    SYNC instead.
 3  Daily session (3 am by default): one quiet trip online that checks the clock, brings in phone, Siri
    and PC changes and commands, fetches the cloud package only if it changed, checks for updates, and
    sends the log and battery report. Phone list changes reach the fridge here, or when you tap SYNC.
 4  The cloud brain does its heavy work every 2 hours and repackages only when something really changed.
    It stays far inside GitHub's free limits.

STANDBY
 5  The clock keeps ticking every minute all night while the Kindle sleeps. Only the changed digits are
    drawn, from ready-made pictures, with no page or Wi-Fi involved, so each tick costs a second or so.
    Settings > Sleep: every minute, every 15 min, hour only or off; a different choice at night.
    This replaces the old home / work / night wake intervals (the night hours are still used).
 6  Every hour the standby page is redrawn cleanly: the weather picture plays a ~10 s animation of the
    current weather, then stays still, and the bird of the hour and the news change.
 7  The battery report: each morning the log, the phone (Fridge tab) and Settings > Sleep show what
    yesterday cost: battery used, wakes, time awake, Wi-Fi trips and seconds, clock ticks.
 8  Battery saver (on by default): below 15% the clock changes every 15 minutes and the animation is skipped.

SCREEN
 9  The left rail always shows awake, battery and last updated. APPS moved to the bottom of the rail; the
    HIDE button is gone (the rail tucks away by itself while asleep).
10  The whole bottom bar is the sleep timer: it fills up as the Kindle gets ready to sleep, with the text
    inverted over the filled part. The bottom bar is now only for app buttons and editing, and its
    buttons are 50% bigger.
11  Editing: tap a tile to select it; its own buttons appear at the bottom (size, move, to page, shuffle,
    hide, options). No more hunting for small handles.
12  The page is quicker: one shared timer instead of a dozen, data re-read only after a sync, and the
    data shown only redrawn when it changed.
