KITCHEN HUB 10.10.5 - THE EDITING SCREEN, THE STANDBY CLOCK, AND OTHER THINGS THAT LOOKED WRONG
Installs by itself like any update: Settings > Updates > CHECK FOR UPDATE.
Things that were visibly wrong, found from photos of the fridge, and the editing screen laid out the way you asked.

WHAT WAS WRONG
 1  THE EDIT BUTTONS WERE INVISIBLE. In 10.10 the whole bottom bar became the sleep timer, so most of
    it is black. The buttons that appear when you tap a tile (S, M, L, XL, MOVE, SHUFFLE...) had black
    edges and black writing, so on the black part of the bar they simply could not be seen; only the
    one chosen size showed. Every button now has its own white face, and the chosen one is black with
    a white edge, so they read on any part of the bar.
 2  A TILE STAYED "BEING EDITED" AFTER YOU PRESSED DONE. The tile you had last tapped kept its
    editing cover for good: on the home page the Calendar was blank with "CHANGE IT IN THE BAR
    BELOW" printed across it. Every tile you tapped after that left another cover stacked behind
    it. They are all removed now, every time.
 3  The "change it in the bar below" note sat on the same line as the tile's name on a wide tile,
    jammed against it. It is on its own line under the name.
 4  The PREV / NEXT buttons said CHEVL and CHEVR (the page flip on the home bar, and the arrows when
    reading a story, a recipe, the day view, the calendar and the horoscope). They say PREV and NEXT.
 5  The title across the top ("GOOD EVENING", "EDITING LAYOUT") lost its last letter. The two empty
    side cells took half the screen, leaving the title only about a quarter of it. The title now has
    the room.

THE EDITING SCREEN, AS YOU ASKED
 6  Tap a tile and its own controls are ON the tile: its size (S M L XL), the two arrows that move it
    along, and the SHUFFLE switch. They are big enough for a finger, and the type shrinks on a small
    tile until they all fit. The bottom bar keeps only what doesn't belong on a tile: TO PAGE 2 / 1,
    HIDE, and ADD / OPTIONS. Tapping a button on the tile no longer also deselects the tile.
 7  The bottom bar is plain white while you edit (the black sleep-timer fill is off; the countdown to
    standby is still in the left rail).
 8  On the home bar the page flip says PAGE 2 or PAGE 1.

THE STANDBY CLOCK WAS SOMETIMES WRONG
 9  The Kindle can wake a second or two before the time it was asked to wake at, and "Hour only" asks
    for the wake 3 seconds early on purpose. The hourly redraw then drew the standby page with the OLD
    hour, so an hour-only clock stayed an hour behind until the next redraw, and a minute clock a
    minute behind until its next tick. A wake now waits out those last seconds first, and so does a
    minute tick that comes early.

THE STANDBY PAGE (ASLEEP) LAYOUT
10  On the upright screen the title was cut to "THE KITCHEN H" (it is shrunk to fit now).
11  The weather line ("Overcast - H 0 L -6") wrapped under the weather picture on the upright screen;
    it stays beside the picture now.
12  The spot where the Kindle draws the clock digits and the weather animation was read in the middle
    of drawing the page, before the weather picture, the bird of the hour and the last fitting of the
    column. On a crowded page the digits and the animation landed a few pixels off the picture
    (over the text beside it). They are read after the page is finished, and again if the bird's
    picture arrives late and the column is fitted again. The bird's picture arriving late also pushed
    the horoscope off the bottom of the column; that is fixed too.

UNDER THE BONNET
13  t26 draws the editing screen in the same old browser the Kindle uses and checks the above (it
    fails on 10.10.4 on fourteen counts). t27 simulates the early wake: the old loop starts every
    redraw 3 seconds before its time, this one starts it on time. t28 draws the standby page at all four
    rotations, plain and crowded, and checks the title, the weather line, overlaps, the column, and
    that the loop is told the true places (it fails on 10.10.4 on twenty-two counts).
