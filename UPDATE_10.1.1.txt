Install: upload the kit to GitHub like before (drag everything, including the .github folder), then on
the fridge Settings > Phone > CHECK FOR UPDATE (tap twice). The phone app updates itself next time you open
it (pull down to refresh if it looks old). New cloud bits (standings, jokes, recipe ideas) show up after
the next cloud brain run.

FIXED
 1  "Every N months" reminders started on the 29th-31st skipped short months (now the last day instead).
 2  Yearly reminders on Feb 29 now land on Feb 28 in non-leap years.
 3  Flyers could "match" a list item to something unrelated when nothing really matched.
 4  New list items waited up to 6 h for a flyer check; now about 30 min.
 5  Hot-deal pushes for deals without a link could only ever fire once.
 6  If the trivia or word of the day failed to load, it wasn't retried until tomorrow (now hourly).
 7  A wishlist item you removed stayed on the Deals tile until the next cloud run.
 8  "+ WATCH" on a hot deal kept junk like " -" and "(YMMV)" in the name.
 9  Phone: a budget typed in lowercase ("groceries") never matched its category.
10  Phone: "My price" could error if the item was removed on the fridge at the same moment.
11  A few emoji (fire, hockey stick, pin) were swapped for symbols the Kindle's fonts are sure to have.
