KITCHEN HUB 10.10.3 - A NIGHT SPENT GOING THROUGH EVERYTHING
Installs by itself like any update: Settings > Updates > CHECK FOR UPDATE.
No new features: this is a pass over the whole hub looking for faults, and what it found.

THE BIG ONE
 1  A WEEK WITH NO SHIFTS FROZE THE HUB. The page tells the background loop when you are at work, and
    when there is nothing in the next seven days it says "no work times at all". The loop wrote that
    down in a way that left the file it hands the page (state.js) broken. From then on the page could
    not read anything from the loop: the countdown stopped, it never learned it was asleep or awake,
    and taps looked dead. Worst of all it was silent - a file that will not load still counts as
    loaded, so nothing appeared in the log. Fixed, and the page now says so in the log if a file from
    the loop ever stops making sense again.

OTHER FAULTS FOUND AND FIXED
 2  After the Kindle refused to go to sleep once (it happens when the screen or Wi-Fi is still busy),
    the hub thought the battery was at 3%, quietly switched to the 15-minute clock and stopped the
    weather animation for the rest of the night.
 3  MAKE NEW KEYS also turned the Kindle on its side: making keys and rotating the screen were asking
    for the same thing by mistake.
 4  If a small timing file was cut off in the middle of being written (a flat battery, a reset), the
    next sync stopped dead at that point: no weather, no news, or a half-finished list sync.
 5  Tapping SYNC in the last seconds before the Kindle nodded off started a sync that the Kindle then
    cut off by sleeping, and could leave Wi-Fi on. Now anything you ask for in those seconds keeps it
    awake, not just a tap on the screen.
 6  The QR button on the shopping list always showed the old picture-of-the-list code instead of the
    live list link for a paired phone.
 7  The battery report's "days left" was worked out from how much history it had, not from the battery.
    The first day's report also had no starting percentage.
 8  Everything the page does on a timer came due at the same moment the Kindle woke up and ran in one
    lump - part of why it felt slow for a second after waking. They are spread out again now.
 9  The battery report now shows in Settings > Sleep the first time you open it, not the second.
10  Smaller things: a story cut off in the middle of an accented character no longer leaves a black
    diamond in the news; a price that isn't a number can't stop the weather and news loading; the file
    with your passphrase keeps its private permissions if it ever needs repairing; the hub clears its
    own leftover listener instead of refusing to start; the weather animation keeps to its ten seconds
    on a slow screen.

UNDER THE BONNET
11  The list code exists in four places (the Kindle page, the phone, the cloud and the fridge's own
    shell). They are now checked against each other automatically, including a four-hundred-line test
    that the shell's merge gives exactly the same answer as the phone's.
12  If anything in the page's state handling ever throws, it is logged once and the page carries on,
    instead of the screen quietly stopping.
